# Linekeep data deletion instructions

Effective date: 6 November 2026

If you have been in contact with Elder Marketing by WhatsApp or phone and want the information we hold about you deleted, follow these steps.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **info@codinfuse.com** with the subject "Delete my data".
2. Write from, or include, the **phone number** we have for you (the number you used on WhatsApp or the number we called), including the country code. We find your records by phone number.
3. We will reply to confirm that we found your records, and we may ask a simple question to confirm the request really comes from you.

You can also send the same request by WhatsApp to our business number.

## What we delete

Within 30 days of confirming your request we delete:

- your customer record: name, phone number, email, location, notes and the employee assigned to you,
- your WhatsApp conversations with us, including pictures, voice notes and files,
- your call records and any call recordings that remain in storage.

Call recordings are in any case removed automatically 30 days after the call.

## What may remain

- Copies held by Meta (WhatsApp) on your own phone and in Meta's systems are outside our control. Manage those in WhatsApp or through Meta's privacy tools.
- Where the law requires us to keep a record (for example invoices or tax records), we keep only what is required, and only for as long as required.
- Backups of our database are overwritten on their normal schedule, so deleted data can remain in a backup for a short time before it disappears.

## Confirmation

When the deletion is complete we email or message you to confirm it.
